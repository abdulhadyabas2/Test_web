Our Philosophy

At Gela, we believe food is medicine, ritual, and joy — all at once. Every dish we craft begins with a question: "Does this truly nourish?"

We partner with local Kurdish farmers and producers to bring you ingredients that are seasonal, organic, and free from compromise. Our kitchen refuses refined sugars, artificial preservatives, and anything your grandmother wouldn't recognize.

Our space is intentionally calm — a refuge from the pace of modern Erbil. Eat slowly. Breathe deeply. Live well.
